Privacy Policy

Your trust is central to our relationship. This policy explains how Aayushman Financial Services collects, uses, protects and handles information shared through our website and services.

01

Our commitment to privacy

Aayushman Financial Services Pvt. Ltd. respects the privacy of visitors, clients and prospective clients. We seek to handle personal information responsibly and only for legitimate business, service, compliance and communication purposes.

By using this website or voluntarily providing information to us, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

02

Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, information may include:

  • Name, contact details, email address and telephone number.
  • Information voluntarily provided through consultation, enquiry, career or contact forms.
  • Information required to provide financial, investment, insurance or related services.
  • Technical information such as browser type, device information, IP address and website usage data.
  • Documents or other information supplied by you where required for onboarding, verification or regulatory purposes.

03

How we use information

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries and consultation requests.
  • Provide, administer and improve our products and services.
  • Complete onboarding, KYC, verification and other applicable compliance processes.
  • Communicate important service, account, regulatory or administrative information.
  • Understand website usage and improve our digital experience.
  • Meet applicable legal, regulatory, audit, fraud-prevention and record-keeping requirements.

04

Sharing & disclosure

We do not sell personal information for advertising purposes. Information may be shared where reasonably necessary to provide requested services, operate our business, comply with applicable law or protect our rights and clients.

This may include regulated financial institutions, technology or service providers, professional advisers, auditors, authorities and other parties where disclosure is legally required or appropriately authorised.

05

Data security

We take reasonable administrative, technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, misuse, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should also take reasonable precautions when communicating sensitive information online.

06

Cookies & website technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support functionality, understand website traffic and improve user experience. You may be able to control cookies through your browser settings.

07

Your rights & choices

Subject to applicable law and regulatory requirements, you may request information about the personal data we hold about you, seek correction of inaccurate information, or raise a privacy-related concern.

Certain information may need to be retained where required by law, regulation, contractual obligations, dispute resolution or legitimate record-keeping requirements.

08

Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, or for periods required by applicable legal, regulatory, tax, accounting or business requirements.

09

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, services, technology or applicable requirements. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
